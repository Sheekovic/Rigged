FIXED RULES, VISIBLE ASSUMPTIONS

A test, with a paper trail.

Initial capital $500 USDT. Margin allocation 10%. Leverage 120×. Profit ceiling +200% of initial margin. Intended loss ceiling −100%. Fibonacci may exit earlier.

The run has not started. Entry tolerance, range timing, margin mode, fees, and funding will be published with its configuration.

Fibonacci version 6 uses the current UTC day's low and high on completed hourly candles. It is not an entry filter. For longs near the low, sell 25% at 23.6%, 38.2%, and 50%; shorts mirror this from the high. Skip targets already behind entry. Keep the initial stop through partial targets. Close the remainder when total net trade profit equals the initial margin, including partial profits, fees, slippage, and funding. A $50 margin therefore targets $50 net profit. A stop at its activation price is an immediate exit. The frozen opposite endpoint can also close the runner. Missing or ambiguous anchors use ROI exits. An entry stop can still lose money after fees and slippage.

Simulation uses completed one-minute candles, not actual exchange fills. If a candle touches both profit and loss levels, the loss is counted first. Liquidation is an approximation, not Binance's exact margin engine. Results can support or contradict the strategy; they cannot guarantee future returns.
